# Get it done by next weekend

Target weekend: **September 5-6, 2026**.

## Sunday, August 30

- Order the exact CYD, Pi Zero 2 W, OTG adapter, and speaker.
- Locate a genuine microSD card, wall supply, power bank, and USB data cable.
- Install Raspberry Pi Imager, VS Code, and PlatformIO.

## Monday

- Flash Raspberry Pi OS Lite 64-bit.
- Boot the Pi on home Wi-Fi and confirm SSH works.
- Run the Pi installer and open the control page.

## Tuesday

- Flash the CYD firmware directly from the computer.
- Complete touch calibration.
- Connect CYD to Pi with the OTG adapter and confirm the test picture appears.

## Wednesday

- Plug in the 8-ohm speaker and test video, sound, touch, and volume for 20
  minutes on wall power.
- Fix any USB cable, controller-profile, or power problems now.

## Thursday

- Upload one authorized playlist.
- Test three channels, channel switching, reconnection, and the phone control
  page. Keep the private playlist out of screenshots and GitHub.

## Friday

- Test on the phone hotspot in 2.4 GHz mode.
- Run from the power bank for at least one hour.
- Attach the Pi, CYD, and battery with a temporary vented mount.

## Saturday

- Take it outside, test signal and battery time, and write down the one or two
  changes needed before designing the permanent 3D-printed enclosure.

## Definition of done

- Boots from one power connection.
- Connects to home Wi-Fi and phone hotspot.
- Plays the included test stream with picture and sound.
- Plays at least one authorized IPTV channel.
- Touch controls work.
- Survives a one-hour battery test without undervoltage or overheating.
