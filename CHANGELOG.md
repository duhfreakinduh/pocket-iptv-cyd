# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-30

- Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W FFmpeg decoder with M3U parsing and reconnection.
- CRC-protected USB-serial JPEG and 16 kHz mono PCM transport.
- ESP32-2432S028R firmware for ILI9341 and ST7789 display revisions.
- Fast 2,000,000-baud and safe 921,600-baud firmware profiles.
- Touch calibration plus on-screen channel, pause/live, and volume controls.
- PIN-protected local phone controller with CSRF protection and private upload.
- One-command Raspberry Pi installer and systemd service hardening.
- Prebuilt, checksummed, merged CYD flash images and easy flash scripts.
- Local Hugging Face Gradio playlist/configuration wizard.
- Shopping list, wiring guide, six-day build plan, and troubleshooting guide.
- Automated Python tests, real FFmpeg media smoke test, and firmware CI builds.
